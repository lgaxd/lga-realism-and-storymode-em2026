# Esports Manager 2026 Mods

Two mods for Esports Manager 2026 (a CS team management game): one that makes the base game more realistic, and one that adds a historical career starting from the early days of competitive CS.

## Language

### Mods

**Realism Overhaul**:
The mod that changes the base game's rules to match how professional CS actually works.
_Avoid_: gameplay mod, main mod

**Story Mode**:
The mod that lets a career start at a historical date, with the real world as it was on that date.
_Avoid_: history mode, career mode, legacy mode

### Teams and squads

**Main Roster**:
A club's first team, the one that competes in the club's primary tournaments.
_Avoid_: main team, first team, A-team

**Academy**:
A club's second team, used to develop young players and feed the Main Roster.
_Avoid_: youth team, B-team, junior team

**Overage Player**:
An Academy player older than the Academy's normal age limit (23). An Academy may have at most one.
_Avoid_: veteran, exception player

### Players and roles

**Role**:
A player's tactical specialty: Entry, Trader, Support, Lurker, AWPer, or Anchor. A player has a primary Role and may have a secondary one.
_Avoid_: position, function, class

**Role Balance**:
How well a lineup's five Roles complement each other: one AWPer, at least one Entry, and a Support or Anchor. A lineup that lacks or doubles key Roles plays slightly worse, and a balanced one slightly better.
_Avoid_: team chemistry, synergy, composition bonus

**IGL**:
The in-game leader, a duty held on top of a Role and not a Role itself.
_Avoid_: captain, shotcaller (as a role)

**CT Position**:
The area of a specific map a player holds on the CT side, such as A-site, Connector, Mid, or B-site. Each map has its own set.
_Avoid_: role, spot, setup

**Map Familiarity**:
How comfortable a player is on a specific map (0–100). It grows with play and practice and fades without them.
_Avoid_: map rating, map skill

**Side Bias**:
Whether a player is stronger on CT or on T, from his Role: Anchors and Supports lean CT, Entries and Traders lean T. A lineup's lean pays off on maps that favour that side.
_Avoid_: CT rating, T rating

**Retired Player**:
A former professional player who no longer plays. Can be hired as a coach at any time, or brought back to play within 5 years of retiring, unless he is employed as staff.
_Avoid_: ex-player, inactive player

**Comeback**:
Bringing a Retired Player back to play, at a premium cost and with a refusal chance that grows with age and time away.
_Avoid_: unretirement, buyout from retirement

**Emergency Sub**:
A coach who used to be a player, filling in as the fifth player for one match when the team, even after calling up the Academy, has fewer than five available. His skills have decayed with the years since he retired.
_Avoid_: player-coach, coach sub

**Performance Slump**:
A sustained drop in a player's results measured against their own recent baseline, not against other players.
_Avoid_: bad form, downtrend, slump (unqualified)

**Roster Suggestion**:
A coach's recommendation, sent in response to a Performance Slump, of up to three replacement candidates who fit the player's Role and the club's budget.
_Avoid_: transfer suggestion, scouting report

### Competition

**Tournament Period**:
The span from a tournament's first scheduled day to its last.
_Avoid_: tournament dates

**Match Day**:
A day on which a team actually has a match in a tournament.
_Avoid_: game day, fixture date

**Double Match Day**:
A day on which a team has two matches, one from each of two overlapping tournaments. They are played one after the other, and the team plays the second one tired. A team is never in more than two tournaments at once.
_Avoid_: match clash, back-to-back matches

**LAN** / **Online**:
Whether a tournament is played in person at a venue or remotely. A team can be at only one LAN at a time.
_Avoid_: offline, remote

**Region**:
One of Valve's three competitive regions: Europe (Europe, CIS, MENA), Americas (North and South America), Asia (Asia, Oceania). Used for VRS.
_Avoid_: continent, zone

**Subregion**:
One of seven areas inside the Regions: Europe, CIS, North America, South America, Asia, Oceania, MENA. A team's Subregion is the majority nationality of its five starters.
_Avoid_: country group, area

**Regional Tournament**:
A tournament open only to teams from one Subregion: one whose name names a Subregion, or an Online T3/T4 tournament (restricted to its host country's Subregion). When a Subregion cannot fill it, teams from the same Region complete the field.
_Avoid_: local tournament, national league

**VRS**:
Valve Regional Standings: the team ranking, modelled on Valve's published method, that decides tournament invites, with the three Regions sharing the slots by quota.
_Avoid_: ranking, HLTV ranking, world ranking

### Story Mode

**Start Date**:
The historical date a Story Mode career begins on. The world matches reality as of that date and nothing after it.
_Avoid_: start year, era

**Historical Fact**:
Something that really happened before the Start Date, such as a ban, a death, or a roster move, and is therefore true when the career begins.
_Avoid_: canon event, scripted event

**Historical Event**:
Something that really happens on a fixed date after the Start Date, regardless of the player's choices: a Game Update, a tournament on the real calendar, an organizer closing down, or the start of VRS.
_Avoid_: scripted event, timeline event

**Game Update**:
A real change to the game itself on a real date, ranging from a weapon nerf to a move to a new game version (1.6 → CS:GO → CS2). It changes how much each Role and weapon matters and briefly disrupts player form.
_Avoid_: patch, balance change, version (unqualified)

**Main Circuit**:
The line of competitive CS that Story Mode follows: CS 1.x/1.6, then CS:GO, then CS2. The parallel CS:Source scene is not part of it.
_Avoid_: scene, competitive history

**Strength Ranking**:
The team ranking that decides invites before VRS existed, estimated from results.
_Avoid_: HLTV ranking, world ranking

**Permanent Leave**:
A player leaving professional play for good, for injury or personal reasons, as a rare random event. Death is never a random outcome; it only appears as a Historical Fact.
_Avoid_: death, random death, forced retirement

**Era**:
One of the five periods of Story Mode history (LAN Party, 1.6 Golden Age, Global Offensive, Esports Boom, CS2). The Era decides which Org Upgrades exist and the economy's scale; the home dashboard shows it.
_Avoid_: age, period, epoch, version (a Game Update is a version change; an Era can span several)

**Org Upgrade**:
Something an organization can adopt once history makes it possible, such as an analyst, a gaming house or a media team, and must pay for. It changes what the organization can do or how well it does it.
_Avoid_: unlock, research, perk, tech
